# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-11

First release. Invoice Ninja with MariaDB and nginx in Container Manager. Your
account is made at the first start, with the password shown once; the
first-start throwaway leaves the environment.

The DSM window adds:
- **Business:** what's owed, what's overdue and what's been paid, in each
  client's currency.
- **Alerts:** a DSM notification when an invoice goes overdue.
- **Users:** new passwords, and two-step sign-in off.
- **Mail:** the mail server, with a test email.
- **Site address:** HTTPS with DSM's certificate.

Also included: consistent backups, updates and removal.
